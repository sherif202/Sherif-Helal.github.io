The 7, one by one

I1

Real total in the cart

Impact 5/5Effort MConfidence high

Automatic shipping estimate from her country; every fee (shipping, cash-on-delivery) named in the cart, so the confirm page shows the same total.

Why (evidence)A flat 7.00 fee appeared only at the last step (+70% on our 10.00 test item). E7 E15 E17
RivalsJumia's cart also hides delivery cost (M1b): a chance to beat a leader.
Where it landsCart, confirm · C3b C3e C3f
Metric · targetOrders where the final total equals the cart total · 100% → Cart → order completion
I2

Delivery promise on the product page

Impact 5/5Effort MConfidence high

Delivery date and cost for her area, with an "order within…" countdown; same promise repeated in the shipping options.

Why (evidence)No delivery date appears anywhere in the flow today. E2 E19
RivalsAll three rivals show it, with a countdown (M2 M4 M6).
Where it landsProduct page, shipping method · C3e C3a C4
Metric · targetProduct page → add-to-cart rate · + (baseline first) → Fewer "leaves to compare" exits
I3

Guest checkout first

Impact 4/5Effort XSConfidence medium

"Checkout as guest" becomes the main button; creating an account is offered on the thank-you page, after the order.

Why (evidence)The guest button is small and grey, and the copy sells registering. E10 E23
RivalsNone of the three rivals allows guest checkout (M1 M3 M5): our edge.
Where it landsSign-in wall, thank-you · C3d
Metric · targetSign-in wall → checkout start · ≥ 90% (example) → Fewer "gives up" exits
I4

Trust content

Impact 4/5Effort XSConfidence high

Real Shipping & Returns page, "free returns within 14 days" on the product page, no placeholder text, no poll, currency symbol on every price.

Why (evidence)Four pages show template placeholder text; prices have no currency. E1 E3 E11 E13 E20
RivalsAmazon and Jumia show the returns promise on the product page (M2 M6).
Where it landsHome, product, sign-in, pickup · C3g C5 C4
Metric · targetPages with placeholder text · 0 → Trust: fewer early exits
I5

Payment clarity early

Impact 4/5Effort SConfidence medium

Accepted methods, card logos and limits on the product page and in the cart; fees named as fees; "Purchase order (business accounts)".

Why (evidence)Payment methods first appear at step 4 of 6. E14 E24
RivalsAmazon flags "electronic payment only" on the product page (M6).
Where it landsProduct, cart, payment · C3f C3a
Metric · targetExits at the payment step · − (baseline first) → Fewer payment-step exits
I6

Shorter checkout

Impact 3/5Effort SConfidence medium

Terms note under the Checkout button instead of tick and pop-up; skip the empty payment-information step; "Edit" links on the confirm page.

Why (evidence)A blocking pop-up and a step with one sentence add friction. E8 E16 E18
Rivals—
Where it landsCart, checkout, confirm · C3c
Metric · targetCheckout steps · 6 → 5 → Faster checkout
