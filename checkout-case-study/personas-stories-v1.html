Personas and user stories v1.0

Written before the evidence, on purpose: v1.0 is our guess, v2.0 is the guess corrected by evidence. The last column shows what the evidence step later found.

Personas

Primary

Nour, doubtful first-time buyer

Usually shops on Amazon. Won't register for one purchase.

Job: “Prove you're safe and worth it before I give you my card.”
Needs: Price vs Amazon, discounts, reviews, delivery date, cost and coverage, accepted payment, returns.
Leaves when: Surprise cost at checkout; card refused at the last step; no reviews.
Secondary

Returning shopper

Has an account, saved addresses and order history.

Job: “Re-order fast and know where my order is.”
Needs: Saved details, one-click repeat, order tracking.
Leaves when: Re-typing details; no tracking.
Enabler

Store admin

Manages products, orders and site text.

Job: “Keep the shop trustworthy without a developer.”
Needs: Editable trust content (shipping, returns, store address).
Leaves when: Placeholder text left live for buyers to see.
Where I'd adjust your model. “A card we don't accept” is a scenario, not a persona: it lives in Edge cases. The guest buyer and Nour are one person, so they were merged.
