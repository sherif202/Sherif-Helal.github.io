Decision log

DecisionChoice and why
SiteDemo Web Shop (demowebshop.tricentis.com), named in the Sprint brief. DemoBlaze was in the UX template only.
Main KRCheckout completion rate. Closest to revenue; one main KR plus a supporting metric (search-to-cart rate).
Evidence methodGoal-anchored expert review + Nielsen heuristics instead of interviews, then cross-checked by running the real AI review plugin (judge 26/30), because users were not reachable. Stated openly in every output.
PersonasGuest buyer merged into Nour: same person, Sherif's version explains why she hesitates. Unsupported card is an edge case, not a persona.
Business buyers (B2B)Option 3: B2C now, B2B later. Keep "Purchase Order" but label it "For business accounts"; full B2B (business accounts, invoices, approvals) parked on the roadmap as Later.
Out of scope for this KRWishlist, compare and order tracking extras, unless the evidence promotes them.
