The 10 stories

S1
SharpenedMust8 ptsI2 · ICE #2
from v1 S1 · E2 E19 · M2 M4 M6

As Nour, I want to see the delivery date and cost for my area on the product page, with an "order within…" countdown, so that I know when it arrives before I compare with Amazon.

Acceptance criteria
  • Given I set my area once, when I open any product, then I see "Arrives by <day>, <date>" and the delivery cost.
  • Given an order cut-off time, when I view the product before it, then I see "Order within Xh Ym".
  • Given I reach the shipping step, then the same date and cost are shown for each option, with no template text.
What changed from v1.0v1 asked for date, cost and coverage. Evidence showed no date anywhere (E19) and all three rivals use a countdown, so the countdown and the repeat in the shipping step were added.
MetricProduct page → add-to-cart rate
S2
MergedMust8 ptsI1 · ICE #5
from v1 S2, S6 · E7 E15 E17

As Nour, I want to see the real total in the cart, with shipping and any cash-on-delivery fee named, so that the total on the confirm page is the same number I saw in the cart.

Acceptance criteria
  • Given my area is known, when I open the cart, then shipping is estimated automatically (no manual estimator).
  • Given I choose cash on delivery, then "Cash-on-delivery fee 7.00" is a named line in the cart and on confirm.
  • Given any fee, when I reach confirm, then the total equals the cart total I last saw.
What changed from v1.0v1 blamed shipping. The walkthrough showed the jump (10.00 → 17.00) came from the payment fee, so v1 S6 (pay another way) was merged in: COD exists, its fee was hidden.
MetricOrders where final total = cart total
S3
SharpenedMust2 ptsI3 · ICE #3
from v1 S3 · E10 · M1 M3 M5

As Nour, I want to continue as a guest from a clear main button on the sign-in page, so that I can buy once without creating an account.

Acceptance criteria
  • Given I click Checkout and I am not signed in, then "Continue as guest" is the primary button, above Register.
  • Given I continue as guest, then I go straight to the address step.
  • The copy no longer sells registering on this page.
What changed from v1.0v1 assumed guest checkout was missing. It exists but is a small grey button (E10). No rival offers it, so it is our edge.
MetricSign-in wall → checkout start
S4
SharpenedShould5 ptsI5 · ICE #4
from v1 S4 · E14 E15 · M6

As Nour, I want to see accepted payment methods, card logos and their fees on the product page and in the cart, so that I don't fill in four steps and then find my way to pay is missing or costs extra.

Acceptance criteria
  • Given I view a product or the cart, then I see the accepted methods with logos.
  • Given a method has a fee, then the fee is written as "fee 7.00", not "(7.00)".
  • Given I reach the payment step, then the options match what I saw earlier.
What changed from v1.0Confirmed: methods first appear at step 4 of 6 (E14). Fees named as fees were added from E15. Moved from Must to Should: ICE rank 4.
MetricExits at the payment step
S5
ReframedMust3 ptsI4 · ICE #1
from v1 S5 · E1 E3 E11 E13 E20 · M2 M6

As Nour, I want to see a returns promise, real shop information and prices with a currency, so that I trust a shop I have never used.

Acceptance criteria
  • Given I view a product, then I see "Free returns within 14 days" linking to a real Shipping & Returns page.
  • Every price shows a currency symbol.
  • No page a buyer can reach shows placeholder text or the poll.
What changed from v1.0Disproved: reviews already exist (3.7 stars, 2,050 reviews, E4). Nour's real trust gap was placeholder text, no returns promise and no currency. Top of the ICE ranking.
MetricEarly exits from product and cart
S6
NewShould3 ptsI6 · ICE #6
new · E8 E16 E18

As Nour, I want to agree to the terms with a short note under the Checkout button, and skip steps that ask nothing, so that checkout feels short and nothing blocks me with a pop-up.

Acceptance criteria
  • Given I click Checkout, then no pop-up blocks me; a line under the button says "By placing the order you agree to the Terms".
  • Given I chose cash on delivery, then the empty payment-information step is skipped.
  • Given I am on confirm, then each section has an Edit link.
What changed from v1.0Not in v1. Found in the walkthrough: a blocking terms pop-up (E8) and a step that only says "You will pay by COD" (E16).
MetricCheckout steps: 6 → 5
S7
ReframedCould3 ptsI3 · ICE #3
from v1 S7 · E23

As Nour (after buying), I want to save my details with one click on the thank-you page, so that next time is faster, without being forced to register before buying.

Acceptance criteria
  • Given I checked out as a guest, when the thank-you page loads, then I see "Save your details for next time" with a password field only.
  • Given I save, then my address from this order is stored.
What changed from v1.0v1 was for the returning shopper. Evidence (E23) showed the natural moment to offer an account is after the first purchase, so it moved to Nour.
MetricGuest → account conversion
S8
KeptShould2 ptsI4 · ICE #1
from v1 S8 · E1 E11 E13

As Store admin, I want to edit shipping, returns, store address and sign-in text in the admin panel, and see a warning when a page still has placeholder text, so that buyers never see unfinished text.

Acceptance criteria
  • Given I open the content pages in admin, then a badge flags any page still holding default template text.
  • Given I save a page, then the buyer-facing page shows the new text.
What changed from v1.0Confirmed by four placeholder findings. Kept as the enabler for S5.
MetricPages with placeholder text: 0
S9
ReframedCould3 ptsI7 · ICE #7
from v1 S9 · E22

As Nour, I want to see the delivery date and next steps on the thank-you page and in the confirmation email, so that I know what happens next without contacting support.

Acceptance criteria
  • Given I place an order, then the thank-you page shows the delivery date and "Confirmation sent to <email>".
  • Given the order is confirmed, then the email repeats the date and how to track.
What changed from v1.0v1 asked for tracking. The real gap is earlier: the thank-you page has no date or email note (E22). Full tracking stays on the backlog.
Metric"Where is my order?" contacts

Why S2 is "Must" but sits 8th. The real total has the biggest impact, but ICE showed it has the least evidence. So the sprint plan should test it cheaply first (count orders where the total changed; a mock-up with 5 buyers), then build it. MoSCoW says what must ship in the release; ICE says what to start first.

INVEST check

I · Independent
S8 (admin) can ship before S5, but S5 needs S8's content: plan them in one sprint.
N · Negotiable
The countdown in S1 can be cut to "Arrives by <date>" if the carrier rules slip.
V · Valuable
Each story names a metric that ties to checkout completion.
E · Estimable
Points come from the backend view (which components change).
S · Small
S1 and S2 are 8 points; split them if the team's sprint can't hold both.
T · Testable
Every story has Given/When/Then criteria a tester can check.
Where I'd adjust your model. Version two of a story is not a reworded version one. Three of the ten changed meaning (S5, S7, S9), one v1 story was disproved, and two new ones came only from walking the flow. That is the point of writing v1.0 before the evidence: the differences are what the review taught us, and they belong in your portfolio.

Story points are our estimates for a small team, to be re-estimated in sprint planning. Persona names are examples.
