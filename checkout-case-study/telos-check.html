What Telos taught us (new evidence)

High E25 · from the critique
Cash on Delivery is pre-selected. The 7.00 fee is not only hidden, the buyer never chose it. Strengthens S2 and improvement I1/I5: default to the no-fee method.
Medium E26 · from the judge
Our walkthrough used a saved address, so a real first-time guest faces a full address form we never captured. Our effort counts understate the pain. Fix: redo the walkthrough in a private window.
Idea From the suggestions
"Place order · 17.00" on the final button, a running total at every step, and tracking drop-off per step to get the KR baseline.
Where I'd adjust your model. Two reviewers agreeing on 7 of 7 problems is good evidence that the findings are real, but it is still expert opinion: on Gilad's Confidence Meter it adds little. What Telos changed is the calibration (it rates friction higher than I did) and two concrete gaps. The honest portfolio line is: "manual review, cross-checked by an AI critique with an independent judge (26/30); both agree; neither replaces users."
