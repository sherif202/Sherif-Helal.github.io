Psychology rules for every screen

  1. Progress, not pressure. Show how close the next step is. Never show a countdown clock.
  2. Protect, don't threaten. Say "keep Gold", not "you will lose Gold". The grace month means no one drops without warning.
  3. Show the money saved. The monthly savings line answers "is talabat Pro at AED 29 cheaper?" without naming it.
  4. Fix before they ask. Late and missing-item credits appear on their own.
  5. Always a free way out. When free deliveries run out, free pickup is the first choice.
  6. Respect Ramadan. Iftar slots say "kept for you", never "discount".
