Free deliveries depend on distance and time

Each 4-week cycle, a top-basket customer gets a number of free deliveries by ring. Off-peak slots are cheapest; events cost the most. After the limit, the customer picks partner delivery, pickup or the standard fee.

Ramadan: sell the slot, not a discount

Courier cost changes through a Ramadan day. Free delivery and cash perks stay in the cheap windows; the iftar window sells reliability at the standard fee.

Rules for Ramadan and Eid

  • No free delivery and no cash perk in the iftar window; the cost is 1.8× and the fee would hit the cap.
  • Gold gets a protected pre-iftar slot, 1 a day, capped at 20% of capacity; unused slots go back to everyone 60 min before.
  • Pre-book before 16:00 for an off-peak drop and earn double streak credit, at no cash cost.
  • The Stage 2 cash perk starts only after Eid, so Ramadan never carries a discount.
  • Make-Good stays on for everyone: lateness at iftar is the moment trust breaks.

How the design answers the market

Customers compare fees and chase promos

Rivals sell free delivery inside paid plans: talabat Pro and Careem Plus at AED 29 a month, Amazon Prime free over AED 25. Keeta competes on low fees.

Answer: no blanket coupons. Free delivery is earned with a basket of AED 185 or an off-peak slot, and the savings ledger shows the AED 29 a month the customer is not paying.

Cash on delivery and mixed payment

In Egypt, cash and bank transfer were 34% of e-commerce payment value in 2025, and 55–70% of shoppers prefer cash.

Answer: cash orders still earn status and credits apply before the rider collects cash. Bonus status points, not money, nudge card and wallet payment.

Ramadan and Eid spikes

Grocery orders rise 33% at iftar and 81% at suhoor (Careem, Ramadan 2026). Courier cost in the event window is about 1.8× off-peak.

Answer: discounts move out of the spike. The iftar window gets protection, not a price cut, and the cost-based fee keeps every order above $0.50.

The time rule protects the money; a cost rise breaks fixed fees

Monthly result for about 2,900 top-basket customers in Dubai, after keeping $0.50 per order. Each row changes one condition or removes one rule.

Fix for the cost rise: set each fee from the drop cost every week, which is what the shared-cost rule in the calculator does.

What is decided and what is explored

Decided in PRD v0.2: tiers, points, Make-Good, Gold perk, AED 9.18 limit, AED 60k budget, no fee. The PRD lists "no free-delivery perk" as a non-goal.

Explored on the assumed-data branch: delivery fees, rings, time slots and the shared-cost rule. These keep free delivery under AED 9.18, so PRD v0.3 can revisit that non-goal once real cost data arrives.
